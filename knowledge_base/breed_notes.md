# Breed Notes

## Golden Retriever
Needs 45-60 minutes of exercise daily, ideally split across two walks.
Prone to hip dysplasia and obesity -- avoid skipping exercise tasks even
when the schedule is tight, and prefer large-breed-appropriate feeding
guidance (see Feeding Guidelines).

## Labrador Retriever
Similar exercise needs to the Golden Retriever (45-60 minutes/day). Highly
food-motivated and prone to overeating -- keep feeding tasks consistent and
avoid adding extra ad-hoc snack tasks.

## Tabby / Domestic Shorthair Cats
Benefit from short enrichment sessions (10-15 minutes, 1-2 times a day)
such as interactive toy play, in addition to regular litter box cleaning
(at least once daily) to discourage litter box avoidance.

## Small Dog Breeds (e.g. Chihuahua, Yorkshire Terrier)
Need shorter but more frequent walks (2-3 short walks of 10-15 minutes)
rather than one long walk, and are more sensitive to being scheduled
outdoors during extreme heat or cold.

## Senior Pets (any breed, 7+ years for dogs / 10+ years for cats)
Prefer a gentler, more consistent daily schedule. Reduce single-session
exercise duration and consider spacing tasks further apart to allow more
rest between activities.
