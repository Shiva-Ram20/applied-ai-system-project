# Feeding Guidelines

## Puppy Feeding (under 1 year)
Puppies should be fed 3 to 4 times a day on a consistent schedule, spaced
roughly 4-6 hours apart. Large-breed puppies (e.g. Golden Retrievers,
Labradors) should be fed a large-breed puppy formula to control growth rate
and reduce the risk of hip and elbow dysplasia.

## Adult Dog Feeding (1-7 years)
Adult dogs are typically fed once or twice a day. Twice-daily feeding
(morning and evening, roughly 8-12 hours apart) is preferred for breeds
prone to bloat, including deep-chested breeds like Great Danes and German
Shepherds.

## Senior Dog Feeding (7+ years)
Senior dogs often do better on smaller, more frequent meals (2-3 times a
day) to ease digestion, and may need feeding tasks spaced away from
medication tasks by at least 30 minutes unless a vet has said otherwise.

## Cat Feeding
Adult cats generally do best with 2 measured meals a day rather than free
feeding, to help manage weight. Kittens under 6 months should be fed 3-4
times a day.

## Medication and Feeding
Unless a specific medication requires food, schedule medication tasks at
least 30 minutes apart from feeding tasks so a missed or late feeding
doesn't cause a missed dose, and vice versa.
